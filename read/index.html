To my wife and boys:

Thank you for the grace and patience you showed me while I was lost in Sawyer’s Reach.

This story is more than just a collection of words on a page. It is a testament to the future we are building together, and a constant reminder of the man I strive to be as a dad, husband, and teacher.

I know we could get through anything together.

This story is for you, with all my love.
