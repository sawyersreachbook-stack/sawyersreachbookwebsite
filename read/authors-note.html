Author’s Note & Closing Message

Thank You for Reading!

Thank you so much for taking the time to read this book. Bringing this story to life has been an incredible journey, and I am deeply grateful to have shared it with you.

As an independent author, word of mouth is the lifeblood of my work. If you enjoyed this book, there are a few quick ways you can help other readers discover it:

  • Leave a Review: A brief, honest review on Amazon, Goodreads, or BookBub makes a massive difference in helping new readers find my work. Even a sentence or two helps immensely!
  • Pass It Along: Recommend this book to a friend, a reading group (I have a discussion guide on my website SawyersReachBook.com), or on social media.

Want to Support Future Projects? I choose to share digital copies of my work freely so that cost is never a barrier to enjoying a good story. If you’d like to support my ongoing writing efforts, you can:

  • Purchase an official print copy on Amazon to add to your bookshelf or gift to a friend.
  • Drop a small tip or buy me a virtual coffee at buymeacoffee.com/nickdudaauthor

Thank you again for your time, your readership, and your support.

Happy reading,

Nick Duda
